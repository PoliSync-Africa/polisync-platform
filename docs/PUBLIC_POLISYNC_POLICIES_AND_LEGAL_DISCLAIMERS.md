## 1. Purpose and Scope

PoliSync Africa is a political technology and information platform intended to support lawful political, electoral, civic, research, observation and organizational activities. These policies govern use of the platform, its websites, applications, APIs, dashboards, communication tools, election-results systems, artificial-intelligence features and related services.

By creating an account, accessing the platform, submitting information or using any PoliSync service, a user agrees to comply with these policies, applicable law, lawful instructions of the platform operator and the rules applicable to the user's organization or role.

---

## 2. Core Platform Principles

PoliSync Africa operates according to these principles:

1. **Legality:** users must use the platform only for lawful purposes.
2. **Accuracy:** users should submit information honestly and should not knowingly fabricate, manipulate or misrepresent electoral or organizational information.
3. **Security:** accounts, authentication credentials and verification codes must be protected.
4. **Privacy:** personal information must be handled only for legitimate, disclosed and authorized purposes.
5. **Role-based access:** users may access only information and actions permitted by their role, organization and approval status.
6. **Auditability:** important administrative, verification and election-result actions may be logged for security, accountability and dispute review.
7. **Neutrality of infrastructure:** platform tools are intended to provide technology and information services; access to a feature must not be interpreted as endorsement of a political party, candidate or political position.
8. **Human oversight:** high-impact decisions must not be based solely on automated or AI-generated output.

## 3. Account Creation and Verification Regulation

### 3.1 Personal accounts
Personal accounts may be self-created subject to the platform's registration, identity, email, phone and security requirements.

### 3.2 Organizational accounts
Political parties, observer organizations, research organizations and other organizational entities are subject to organizational registration and approval procedures defined by PoliSync.

### 3.3 Super Admin
The Super Admin is a privileged platform role with system-level administration authority. The Super Admin account is subject to enhanced security controls and verification.

### 3.4 Other administrators
National, regional, constituency, polling-station and other organizational administrators must be associated with the appropriate approved organization and must use the platform only within their authorized scope.

### 3.5 Verification
A verification badge or verified status represents a platform verification state and must not be represented as government accreditation, electoral commission certification or legal professional certification unless the platform expressly states that such external certification has been independently confirmed.

### 3.6 One person, one account principle
Users must not create deceptive duplicate identities, impersonate another person or use another person's credentials.

## 4. Acceptable Use Regulations

Users must not:

- impersonate a person, organization, candidate, election official or public institution;
- submit knowingly false election results or supporting documents;
- alter, conceal or destroy records to mislead users or investigators;
- interfere with polling-station, constituency, regional or national workflows without authorization;
- attempt to bypass authentication, OTP, access controls or role restrictions;
- introduce malware, malicious code or harmful content;
- scrape or export protected personal data without authorization;
- use the platform to facilitate fraud, bribery, intimidation, threats, harassment or unlawful discrimination;
- use AI features to generate deceptive documents or knowingly false claims presented as verified facts;
- misuse communication tools for spam, phishing or unlawful campaigning;
- reverse engineer, attack or intentionally overload the platform;
- use another organization's or user's confidential information without lawful authorization.

PoliSync may suspend, restrict or terminate access where there is reasonable evidence of serious policy or security violations, subject to applicable law and any applicable review process.

## 5. Electoral Results Submission and Verification Regulations

### 5.1 Authorized submission
Election results may be submitted only by users authorized for the relevant polling station or organizational workflow.

### 5.2 Data integrity
A submitter is responsible for entering figures accurately and reconciling totals before submission. The platform may reject mathematically inconsistent submissions.

### 5.3 Transmission hierarchy
Where enabled, submitted results may be transmitted through the relevant organizational hierarchy, for example from polling-station operations to constituency, regional and national organizational dashboards.

Transmission through PoliSync is a **technical delivery mechanism** and is not itself a declaration that a result is officially certified.

### 5.4 Verification states
Results may be marked, according to the workflow, as pending, verified, discrepancy, disputed or rejected. These states describe the platform's processing state and do not automatically constitute official electoral certification.

### 5.5 Supporting documents
Where documents such as EC8/pink-sheet materials are processed, extracted information may be compared with manually entered figures. AI or automated extraction may contain errors and must be reviewed by authorized humans before consequential decisions.

### 5.6 Public results
Publicly displayed results must identify their source, status and relevant election context where available. Unverified, estimated, projected or partial information must not be presented as officially certified results.

## 6. Political Party and Organizational Regulations

Each political party or organization is responsible for:

- maintaining authorized administrators;
- controlling internal invitations and access codes;
- ensuring users assigned to regions, constituencies and polling stations are properly authorized;
- maintaining accurate organizational profile information;
- complying with applicable election, political-finance, privacy and communications laws;
- correcting inaccurate organizational records without attempting to conceal the audit trail.

Organizational hierarchy permissions do not authorize a user to access another organization without lawful permission.

## 7. Privacy and Personal Data Regulation

PoliSync should collect and process personal information only for identified and legitimate purposes, such as account creation, authentication, security, organizational administration, lawful communication, service delivery and auditability.

Depending on the feature and applicable law, information may include names, contact details, profile images, organizational roles, location information, device/security information, communications metadata, election-submission records and account activity logs.

### 7.1 Location data
Where a user grants location permission, PoliSync may use location information to provide location-aware services such as weather or relevant geography. The platform should display human-readable location names where practical and should not unnecessarily expose precise coordinates in the user interface.

### 7.2 Data minimization
Only data reasonably required for the relevant feature should be collected, retained and displayed.

### 7.3 User rights
Subject to applicable law, users may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability or withdrawal of consent. Requests should be submitted through the platform's designated privacy channel.

### 7.4 Security incidents
Where required by law, PoliSync will investigate and provide legally required notifications concerning qualifying security incidents.

## 9. Artificial Intelligence Policy

PoliSync may use AI models for assistance with analysis, summarization, document processing, research support, election-intelligence tools and other permitted features.

AI-generated output:

- may be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated or biased;
- must not be treated as an official electoral determination;
- should be independently reviewed before consequential use;
- should not be presented as human-authored or independently verified when it is not;
- must not be used to fabricate electoral evidence or impersonate a person or institution.

The AI analyzer is an assistance and intelligence tool. It is not a substitute for an election commission, court, legal adviser, auditor, journalist, researcher or other competent authority.

## 10. Security Regulations

Users must keep passwords, OTPs, recovery codes, API credentials and session tokens confidential.

PoliSync may require periodic re-verification, including phone OTP verification after defined security or session events. Users must promptly report suspected account compromise.

Administrative privileges are subject to least-privilege principles. The platform may record administrative actions for security and audit purposes.
