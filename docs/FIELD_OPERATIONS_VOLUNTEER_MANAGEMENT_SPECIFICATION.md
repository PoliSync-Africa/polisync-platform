# POLISYNC AFRICA — Field Operations & Volunteer Management Specification

**Version:** 0.1 (Founder Blueprint)

---

# Purpose

The Field Operations & Volunteer Management module coordinates campaign activities from national headquarters to polling stations by managing volunteers, field officers, polling agents, logistics, attendance, and real-time field reporting.

This ensures campaign leaders know who is working, where they are, and what is happening on the ground.

---

# Objectives

- Manage volunteers.
- Deploy polling agents.
- Coordinate field teams.
- Track attendance.
- Monitor GPS check-ins.
- Report incidents in real time.
- Manage campaign logistics.

---

# User Roles

- National Operations Director
- Regional Coordinator
- Constituency Coordinator
- Electoral Area Coordinator
- Polling Station Coordinator
- Volunteer
- Polling Agent
- Observer

---

# Volunteer Management

Every volunteer has a profile containing:

- Volunteer ID
- Full Name
- Phone Number
- WhatsApp
- Skills
- Languages Spoken
- Assigned Area
- Availability
- Training Status
- Performance Score

# Field Team Structure

Teams can be organized by:

- Region
- Constituency
- Electoral Area
- Polling Station
- Special Campaign Unit

Each team has:

- Team Leader
- Deputy Leader
- Members
- Assigned Tasks

---

# Task Assignment

Assign tasks with:

- Task Title
- Description
- Location
- Priority
- Deadline
- Assigned Member

Statuses:

- Pending
- In Progress
- Completed
- Cancelled

- Houses Visited

- Voter Concerns

- Follow-Up Needed
