# POLISYNC AFRICA — Fundraising & Finance Management Specification

**Version:** 0.1 (Founder Blueprint)

---

# Purpose

The Fundraising & Finance Management module provides political parties, candidates, and civic organizations with a secure financial operating system for collecting donations, managing campaign budgets, tracking expenditures, approving payments, maintaining audit trails, and generating financial reports.

The system is designed for Africa's financial ecosystem, with support for Mobile Money, bank transfers, cards, and modern payment gateways.

---

# Objectives

- Collect donations securely.
- Manage campaign budgets.
- Track expenditures.
- Approve financial requests.
- Generate financial reports.
- Maintain transparent audit trails.
- Support multiple African payment systems.
- Detect financial risks using AI.
