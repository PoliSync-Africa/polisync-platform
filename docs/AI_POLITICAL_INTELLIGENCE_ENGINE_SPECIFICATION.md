# POLISYNC AFRICA — AI Political Intelligence Engine Specification

**Version:** 0.1 (Founder Blueprint)

---

# Purpose

The AI Political Intelligence Engine transforms POLISYNC AFRICA into an intelligent political operating system that helps parties, candidates, researchers, and civic organizations make data-driven decisions before, during, and after elections.

Instead of simply storing data, the system analyzes patterns, predicts trends, identifies risks, and recommends actions.

---

# Core Objectives

- Predict political trends.
- Identify swing constituencies.
- Measure campaign effectiveness.
- Detect election risks.
- Generate automatic strategic reports.
- Provide leaders with real-time intelligence.
